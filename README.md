## 🌟 The Vision: Why Traditional Stores Fail & How This Stays Up Forever

Starting a clothing brand traditionally costs \$5,000–\$20,000 in upfront bulk garments, screen printing minimums, and warehouse storage. SaaS storefronts like Shopify demand \$39–\$299/month regardless of whether you make a sale.

**Atelier Threads flips the script with a 100% Free, Zero-Risk Architecture:**
1. **$0 Hosting Forever:** Deployed to **GitHub Pages edge CDN** with free SSL, infinite global uptime, and zero recurring server or subscription costs.
2. **Zero-Inventory Cashflow:** Customers fund the manufacturing. When someone buys a \$78 heavyweight hoodie, their \$78 arrives in your account first. You automatically route the \$29 wholesale manufacturing cost to Printify/Gelato—pocketing **\$49 pure profit** with \$0 capital risked.
3. **Dual Model Power:**
   - **POD Model (Your Own Designs):** 280 GSM oversized tees, 460 GSM brutalist hoodies, acid-wash crewnecks, and canvas totes printed direct-to-garment (DTG) with bio-inks only when ordered.
   - **Curated Dropship Model:** High-margin avant-garde techwear pants, modular chest rigs, rimless titanium shades, and surgical steel chains sourced from vetted global suppliers with automated dispatch.

## 📖 The $0 Operating Blueprint

### Step 1: Connect Your Free POD Provider
1. Create a free account at [Printify.com](https://printify.com/) or [Gelato.com](https://gelato.com/).
2. For India-specific local fulfillment, register free on [Blinkstore.in](https://blinkstore.in/) or [Qikink.com](https://qikink.com/).
3. Paste your free API token into the **Merchant Admin Portal** (`Settings` tab).

### Step 2: Connect Your Payment Gateways
1. Register a standard free account at [Stripe.com](https://stripe.com/) or [Razorpay.com](https://razorpay.com/).
2. Grab your publishable key / key ID and paste it into the admin settings.
3. Every customer payment lands in your bank account before wholesale costs are deducted.

### Step 3: Zero-Ad-Spend Organic Marketing
- **TikTok & Reels:** 7-second aesthetic garment showcases paired with trending audio.
- **Pinterest Boards:** Curated moodboards with outbound direct links to your product pages.
- **Reddit & Discord:** Share custom design drops in streetwear and techwear subreddits (`r/streetwearstartup`, `r/techwearclothing`).
